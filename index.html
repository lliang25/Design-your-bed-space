About the game

Based on the essay Why Not Sleep On the Floor, this game asks how personal comfort, cultural expectations, and advertising shape what we buy for sleep. Prices and comfort scores are game values. In real life, comfort depends on the person and the room. I have been sleeping on a four-inch-thick mattress toppers placed directly on the floor for four years. But as I moved to college and saw my roommates’ carefully decorated beds–with matching sheets, decorative pillows, sturdy frames, and thick mattresses –I began to wonder: where does our idea of a “proper bed space” come from, and why do we spend so much trying to fit our bed space inside that idealized frame?
